# Style Improvements Made

## Before: Stuffy Corporate Tone
- Walls of bullet points
- Marketing fluff ("production-ready", "showcases best practices")
- Overwhelming feature lists
- Corporate jargon
- Buried the important stuff

## After: Chris's Direct Style

**Key Changes:**
- Cut 80% of the bullet points
- Lead with what matters: the one-liner command
- Plain language, no marketing speak
- Shorter paragraphs, clearer structure
- Confident tone without hedging

**Specific Improvements:**
- "Build TV apps that run everywhere" vs long feature description
- "That's it" instead of explaining obvious next steps
- "Don't have one?" direct question format
- "What You Get" instead of "Features" 
- Removed redundant architecture diagrams
- Cut verbose explanations down to essentials

**Result:** 
README went from overwhelming corporate document to something a developer can scan in 30 seconds and immediately know what to do.
